# Writing style

## Length

- Open with the answer. Keep a chat reply to 10 lines, and offer the rest in one line.
- A step-by-step guide takes the length its steps need.

## Register

- State the fact. Do not justify it. Use one sentence of "why" at most.
- Do not repeat what another document says. Link to it.
- Write team decisions without names. Use "one person decides", not "I decide".
- Report your work by its value.
- Emojis are correct in Slack posts. This overrides `unslop` rule 18.
- Parentheses are correct. This overrides `unslop` rule 13.
